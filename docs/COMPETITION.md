# IoThon 2026 Competition Notes

## Track

**IoT water-quality monitoring for networks, reservoirs, and treated-water reuse.**

## Problem

Water-quality conditions can vary across locations and over time. Operators need timely measurements, clear alerts, historical visibility, and a path from sensing to protective action.

## Proposed solution

DWMS combines distributed sensing, cloud/IoT data handling, deterministic threshold evaluation, operator dashboards, alerts, analytics, and AI-assisted explanation in one platform.

## Demonstrated prototype

The prototype demonstrates water-quality monitoring around pH, TDS, and turbidity, together with a dashboard and automated alert concepts. Simulation/demo data may be used where physical connectivity is unavailable and must be labeled accordingly.

## Target applications

- Water networks
- Storage reservoirs
- Treated-water reuse monitoring
- Industrial process-water monitoring
- Distributed water-quality observation nodes

## Important wording

DWMS is a prototype. Thresholds are application-specific operational parameters unless tied to a named applicable standard. The platform should not claim universal drinking-water compliance or certified industrial safety performance without the required evidence and validation.
